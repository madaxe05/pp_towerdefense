Realm Guard: Monster Siege TD Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026 · Developer: Sohan Dhungel

Summary

Realm Guard: Monster Siege TD ("the game") does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information. It works fully offline.

What the game collects

Nothing.

  • No account, sign-in or email is required.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs are included.
  • No internet connection is used. The game makes no network requests.
  • No device identifiers, location, contacts, camera, microphone or files are accessed.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, towers, skills, settings, daily rewards and achievements) is saved only in the game's private storage on your device. It never leaves the device. Uninstalling the game or using "Reset Progress" in Settings deletes it.

Permissions

  • Vibrate: used for optional haptic feedback. You can turn it off in Settings.

Children

The game contains no ads, no in-app purchases and no data collection, and is suitable for all ages.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data (for example cloud saves or ads), this policy will be updated before that version is released.
